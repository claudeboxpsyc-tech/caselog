# Case Log: getting started

Case Log tracks your operations against the IMA urology syllabus and makes the Excel lists for
the specialist title. It runs in your browser, works offline, and keeps your cases on your own
devices. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you pair a second device, and then only encrypted.

## 1. Install it (2 minutes per device)

Open the link you were sent, then:

- **iPhone:** open it in **Safari**, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**. From now on, **always open
  Case Log from that icon**. Safari can clear data of websites that aren't on the home screen,
  and your cases live in the app's storage.
- **Android:** open it in Chrome, tap **⋮ → Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
- **Computer:** Chrome and Edge show an install button at the right of the address bar. Safari on
  a Mac: **File → Add to Dock**. Or just bookmark it.

## 2. Pair your phone and computer (once)

1. On your **phone**: **More → Set up sync on this device**. A QR code appears.
2. On your **computer**: **More → Pair with my other device**.
   - With a camera: point it at the phone's QR code.
   - Without one: on the phone tap **Copy code**, send it to yourself, and paste it in.
3. Done. Every case you save on one now appears on the other within a minute or so.

The pairing code unlocks your whole log. Use it only on your own devices and don't send it
through shared chats; if you message it to yourself, delete the message afterwards.

To add a third device (a tablet, a new phone), use **More → Pair another device** on one that's
already paired.

## 3. Log a case

**Log** tab: tap the procedure (your most-used ones rise to the top), fill in record number or
initials and age, and tap **Save**. Primary surgeon is the default role.

- Mark everything done in the operation; Case Log counts it under the most significant procedure
  whose syllabus slots aren't full yet. Obvious companions (cystoscopy with TURBT or TURP) are
  added for you.
- No signal in the OR? Save anyway. It syncs when you're back online.

**Progress** shows how far you are in each category. **Cases** lets you search, edit or delete.

## 4. Get your lists for the specialist title

**More → Your name on the lists**: enter your name once. Then **Download Excel lists** gives the
IMA summary, the detailed list by type, and every case in one table.

## Keeping your log safe

- Your cases are stored on each of your devices. Two paired devices are copies of each other, so
  losing one loses nothing.
- Now and then, **More → Download backup (CSV)** and keep the file somewhere safe. It's your copy
  that doesn't depend on any device.
- Clearing your browser's data for this site, or deleting the home-screen app, removes the cases
  on that device. If it was paired, pair it again and they come back from your other device.
- Don't screenshot or share screens with patient details, as with any patient record.
